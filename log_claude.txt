[2026-05-25] Paper specifics (from arXiv 2605.19376v2 abstract + HTML section D.5)
- Full name: GRAM = Generative Recursive reAsoning Models. Extends deterministic RRMs.
- Method: stochastic latent trajectory; amortized variational inference (encoder q_phi + prior p_theta).
- Loss: E_q[log p(y|z_T,x)] - KL(q_phi(eps_T|u_T,y) || p_theta(eps_T|u_T)).
  Sudoku: beta_KL=0.1, KL-balance=0.8.
- Sudoku-Extreme architecture for GRAM:
  * Recursive Core swaps Attention+SwiGLU -> [SwiGLU + SwiGLU] (Sudoku-only).
  * Hidden D=512, 10.9M params total.
  * State z=(h, l); K=6 low-level refinements; T=3 high-level transitions; N_sup=16 supervision steps.
- Training: AdamW, lr=1e-4, batch=768, ~50K epochs (200 for unconditional), 8x RTX 4090, ~2h.
- Results: 97.0% cond. accuracy on Sudoku-Extreme; 99.05% validity unconditional gen.
- Baseline RRM = TRM (TinyRecursiveModels @ SamsungSAILMontreal, MIT).
  TRM Sudoku: arch.mlp_t=True, L_layers=2, H_cycles=3, L_cycles=6, lr=1e-4, 50K epochs, ~87% acc.

[2026-05-25] Paper appendix recheck for collapse tricks
- Confirmed Table 7: beta_kl = 0.1 for Sudoku; KL balance = 0.8.
- Paper does NOT use KL warmup or free-bits — relies entirely on KL balance.
- Paper DOES use two auxiliary losses we currently OMIT:
    L_ACT  = sum [(q_halt^n - q_halt_hat^n)^2 + (q_continue^n - q_continue_hat^n)^2]
            (paper Eq. 15; note MSE form vs TRM's BCE on q_halt only)
    L_LPRM = sum (v_psi(z_t) - r)^2     (paper Eq. 16; predicts reward from latent)
- The auxiliary losses give additional gradient signal that ties z (and thus eps)
  to a non-redundant prediction task, helping prevent posterior collapse.
- Implication: even if our KL warmup+free_bits hack works numerically, a more
  faithful repro should re-introduce these aux losses. For now, anti-collapse
  smoke tests the warmup path first.
